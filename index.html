Our Story — Since 1990

A school built on faith,
discipline & service.

Established in 1990 at Tuishimi Village, a few minutes walk east of NH-39, Mahatma Gandhi Evergreen Higher Secondary School began with just 45 pupils and 5 teachers (K.G. to Class IV) in a rented thatch house.

By grace and grit it grew: recognised as a Junior High School in 1992, upgraded to Class-X to shelter displaced Naga students during the Naga-Kuki conflict, recognised by the Board of Secondary Education, Manipur in 2000, first HSLC batch in 2002, HSLC Private Examination Centre for Pallel Town in 2005, and upgraded to Higher Secondary (Science Stream) recognised by COHSEM in 2010.

Rooted in the life and teachings of Jesus Christ, the school imparts practical education and sound moral training — discipline, virtue and service — shaping good and responsible citizens.

Message from the Director

My dear Learners & Parents,

It was my great pleasure to launch the school website on 10th December 2017 — a day we now celebrate every year as “School Transformation Day.” I thank Mr. Dinesh Pradhan of Pallel Town for designing it and bringing it to reality.

Vision • Objectives • Values

Education for Human Excellence.

To build mental processes that make a learner a holistic human being — emotionally stable, intellectually vibrant, spiritually committed: a ‘whole man’.

◈

Academic Excellence

Rigorous learning from K.G. to Class XII, BSEM + COHSEM Science stream, HSLC examination centre, career counselling and merit scholarships for class toppers.

◎

Physical Excellence

Sports & cultural promotion, football, volleyball, badminton, basketball and future swimming facilities — a healthy body for a vibrant mind.

✦

Spiritual Insight

Love, sacrifice and service. Discipline, virtue and moral training that nurtures self-respect and righteous citizenship.

Objectives: to draw out academic excellence, physical excellence and spiritual insight towards building a beautiful personality; to strengthen innate human potential, nurture the righteous path, and groom responsible, productive citizens of the country.
